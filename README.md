. INTRODUCTION
A.1 Objectives:
The objectives of outlining and implementing this Change Policy are to:
A1.1. provide standardised methods and procedures to meet the Change
Management Requirements to support the Company’s operations;
A1.2. eliminate uncontrolled changes to IT systems, infrastructure and applications which
could potentially result in significant system disruption, data corruption or data loss.
A formalised IT Change Management Process is designed to ensure
that changes are authorised and that changes operate as intended;
A1.3. minimise the risk and severity of any disruption;
A1.4. ensure that all changes are logged (or captured) in a centralised data
repository, from where the information can be shared by other processes;
A1.5. systematically assess all changes based on their impact, benefit and risk to
the Business, and to approve or reject the Request for Change accordingly;
A1.6. schedule all changes based on the requirements of the Business, the availability
of appropriate resources, and in consideration of other changes being deployed;
A1.7. ensure that all changes are appropriately tested and certified; and that
the appropriate implementation and remediation plans are available, and…
A1.8. provide general reporting capabilities against the repository of changes,
including trending information and specific metrics relevant to the process.
 
A.2 Scope of this Change Policy:
A2.1. This Change Policy will apply to all IT Systems, Infrastructure or
Applications managed by Rain (Pty) Ltd. (Hereafter referred to as ‘rain,’)
and to all of rain’s Subsidiaries, Vendors and Contractors that store, process
or transmit information, including network and computer hardware, software
and applications, mobile devices, and telecommunication systems.
A2.2. This Change Policy will apply to all formal change requests to IT Systems,
Infrastructure and Applications, including Work Orders, Normal Planned, Standard
and Emergency Changes. (See Section B.2 for categories of Change Requests.

B. DEFINITIONS
B.1 General Definitions:
B1.1. ‘rain’ - Rain Group Holdings (Pty.) Ltd, and any subsidiaries thereof.
B1.2. ‘CAB’ - Change Advisory Board. This is a group of people that make decisions related to
impactful changes in IT Systems. Members of the CAB may include the CEO,
various GMs of the relative departments, IT Managers, and Owners of system(s)
affected by the change. The chair of the CAB is usually the Change Manager.
B1.3. ‘eCAB’ - Emergency Change Advisory Board. This is a group of people that make
decisions related to High Risk / High Impact emergency changes to IT Systems.

B2.2.
A ‘Normal Planned Change’ is a change that carries
Medium or High Risk / Impact characteristics.
These changes must be assessed for unforeseen impact by
all Technical Reviewers in each department, and then presented
through the Change Advisory Board (CAB) for approval.
 
B2.3. Standard Change:
A ‘Standard Change’ is a change that has a pre-defined,
documented and pre-approved Change Implementation Plan.
These are pre-approved and do not require formal approval to implement.
 
B2.4. Emergency Change:
An ‘Emergency Change’ is an unplanned modification to an IT System that
requires immediate implementation to correct an important issue, such as a
disruption or outage of service, or to introduce security or regulatory compliance.
This will be submitted to the Emergency Change Advisory Board. (eCAB)
